## Introduction
A lot of the presentations we've seen so far have involved the internet, 
which makes sense. When you or I think of computer networking, its easy to 
think of the juggernaut that is the modern internet. But today I'm going to 
ask you a question that was asked on the first day of class. What do you do
when the internet goes down? Living here in the U.S. it is easy to find this
question mundane. I'd read a book, that was my answer. But for many peole 
across the globe it is a question with a lot more weight. For countries such 
as x, y, and many more, rolling blackouts is the day-to-day. As climate 
change forces more and more countries to grapple with the reality that 
constant internet access may not always be a guarantee, we must take weight in
the question: How would we communicate if the internet goes down?
